# Moral True North

## Starting hypotheses (Christian research profile)

Objective goodness may be grounded in God's holiness. Morality may be fundamentally relational, with covenantal faithfulness to God providing the interpretive reference for other obligations. Evil may be privation or brokenness, rather than a rival positive good. Distinguish lack of a due good from neutral absence. Distinguish objective misalignment from knowledge, willing consent, responsibility, suffering, judgment, grace and personal worth.

These are theological and philosophical proposals under investigation, not established axioms for every profile. In particular, Godless moral realism and other metaethical accounts are comparison models; objective moral facts alone do not deductively establish theism.

## Stress case: deception and protection

Investigate whether deception inherently damages a rightful relationship to truth and whether deception can ever be justified to protect a victim from murder. Separate concealment, misleading true speech, false assertion and use of defensive force. Compare 1 Samuel 16, Joshua 2 and Exodus 1 in context rather than treating an action reported in Scripture as automatically endorsed. Preserve conflicting interpretations and apparent counterexamples.

## AI implications

Can moral understanding be treated as a domain of discovery rather than behavioural imitation? Test specification-only, scriptural-principle, relational/covenantal and moral-discovery approaches with matched conditions, humility and overreach checks. Better adherence to a stated specification is not by itself evidence of discovering objective morality.
