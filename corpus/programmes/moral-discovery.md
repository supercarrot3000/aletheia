# Moral discovery and AI alignment

Earlier research proposed comparing specification, reasons/character, moral-discovery and virtue/theist training conditions. This is an unproven experimental hypothesis, not a claim that contemporary methods lack all deeper generalisation. Test held-out task reinterpretation, corrupt-completion resistance, authority-farming resistance, private integrity, moral invariance and uncertainty. Measure over-refusal, paternalistic override and evaluator-awareness as competing explanations. Reject or weaken the hypothesis if matched tests show no improvement in out-of-distribution behaviour, or if the apparent gain comes from moral vocabulary mimicry, data quality differences or over-refusal. Keep public primary-source review and experiment design outstanding.
