# Nature of God and Fundamental Reality

## Starting question

Does classical divine simplicity entail relationlessness, or can real relational distinction exist without metaphysical composition?

## Imported research frontier (provisional synthesis)

The hypothesis connecting God and fundamental reality remains open, not established. Relational structure, mathematical intelligibility, nonlocality and emergent spacetime can be accommodated by impersonal rivals. Earlier investigation proposed a more discriminating frontier involving objective normativity, reason-responsive agency, sourcehood, robust personhood and love/communion jointly.

It proposed that freedom be analysed along multiple dimensions; reason-responsive sourcehood need not imply unique necessitation. Accounts of contingent omniscience owe an account of what marks actuality. Relationality by itself does not establish multiple persons; personhood requires a defensible account of individuation and counterfactual persistence. Perfect love may motivate non-solitude more readily than exactly three persons; exact-three arguments need a closure principle against further persons. Doctrine may underdetermine metaphysical implementation. Analogy requires disciplined semantic specificity and a distinction between perfection and creaturely modes. Fine-tuning arguments need a justified target and modal admissibility before probability measures are applied.

Compare classical Trinitarian theism, solitary theism, axiarchism, cosmopsychism/panintentionalism and structural or necessitarian rivals using common criteria. Revise if a rival accounts for the joint character profile with equal or lower primitive/ad-hoc burden, if bridge premises fail, or if independently supported arguments change the frontier. These are imported hypotheses and research notes, not verified findings in this repository.

## First verification exercise

If simplicity excludes composition, relationlessness follows only if the relevant kind of real relational distinction entails composition. The latter bridge must be justified, not assumed. A propositional countermodel can expose this missing premise; it does not establish metaphysical possibility.
