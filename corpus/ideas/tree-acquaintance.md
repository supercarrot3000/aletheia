# IDEA-002 — The tree and knowledge by acquaintance

**Kind:** analogy; contributor-supplied. **Status:** candidate interpretation; not evidence of the metaphysical hypothesis.

“The difference between seeing and smelling and touching a tree and only reading about one.”

The analogy distinguishes descriptive knowledge from first-person acquaintance. A system might describe a tree in extraordinary detail without directly experiencing it. It prompts an analogous question about moral beauty, recognition and experience. It does not establish that artificial systems or animals lack experience, nor that knowledge by acquaintance is a necessary condition for every kind of moral understanding or choice.
