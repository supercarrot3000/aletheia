## Acceptance criteria

1. Publish the reviewed identity investigation as a draft PR. Give a first-time reader a plain-language entry point and a path to the argument, source, evidence and unresolved objection.
2. Provide an offline worksheet and a worked example for exploring a new idea. Provide a stable, machine-readable finding with versioned inputs, scope and replay instructions. Show one valid transfer outside theology and one near-match where transfer fails.
3. Use Lean and Mathlib to check when a relation descends to equivalence classes and why an opposed pair in one class prevents descent. Supply both a positive and a negative transfer example and replay the existing Z3 witnesses.
4. Use Isabelle/HOL and Nitpick on Q-GOD-07. Replay a specified computational-theology reference result and test a separate Aletheia modal argument with explicit frames, existence checks and independently inspectable finite evidence.
5. Keep tool-specific checks optional for ordinary corpus use. Document exact installation and replay, verify all substantive runs, and keep review of applicability visible.
