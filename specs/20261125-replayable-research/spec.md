# Replay a research argument and expose its missing bridge

Status: initial assignment, local implementation for owner review

## Problem

The seed links ideas, claims and questions informally. The tests cover seven fixture verdicts, unique IDs and some file and source references. The formal exercises are disconnected from the corpus. The runner is a design, and the draft has no Git metadata or protected acceptance channel.

## Outcome

A researcher can open an attributed idea in full, trace the divine-simplicity exercise to its definitions, premises, claim, objection and question, and replay its verification from a local checkout. A proposal author and an independent verifier can use different directories.

## Acceptance tests

1. Given the seed, the Purple Man narrative, clarifications, unresolved attribution and acknowledgement remain byte-identical. A local reader can retrieve the full idea by ID.
2. Given the divine-simplicity gap and bridge control, verification reports a concrete countermodel for the gap and checks the bridge entailment in pinned Lean 4. The report exposes the assumptions, and the corpus retains the open objection about semantics.
3. Given malformed corpus data, invalid typed references or circular argument dependencies, the verifier fails with a diagnostic. Seed regressions and targeted mutations exercise these boundaries and the logical checks.
4. Given an independently retained trusted copy and a proposal, the verifier runs the trusted checker and policy, detects edits to the required seed controls, and records content hashes and the verification results. A receipt replay against different inputs fails.
5. Given a question ID, a human or external AI can receive a deterministic, self-contained research packet for that question. The same local workflow works without provider credentials, private services or Git metadata.
6. The README explains the Christian origins and invitation to challenge, the roles and status of Lean 4, Mathlib, Isabelle/HOL, Nitpick and Z3, and relevant computational theology. Durable documentation explains the audit, operational limits and remaining stages.

## Release boundary

The draft stays local. Public release requires owner review of the tree, history, licensing and rights. The exercise keeps theorem validity, premise support, experience and theological interpretation distinct.
