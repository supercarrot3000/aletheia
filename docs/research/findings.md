## Does sharing an essence imply identity?

**Answer.** If two things are each identical to the same thing, they are identical to each other. An encoding in which they share an essence label permits them to remain distinct and opposed. The stronger theological question remains open. Aquinas describes sameness in reality and distinguishes the relations by their opposition. A common label may be too weak to capture that account. [Investigation and replay](../../corpus/investigations/simplicity-identity.md) · [Primary source, I, q. 28, a. 3](https://www.corpusthomisticum.org/sth1028.html)

## When does grouping preserve a relationship?

**Answer.** A relationship can be defined on groups exactly when swapping either item for another in its own group preserves the answer. If an item cannot stand in the relationship to itself and two grouped items stand in the relationship to each other, grouping erases a distinction the relationship needs.

**Valid reuse.** Group catalogued objects by colour. “Has the same colour as” gives the same answer after replacing either object with another of its own colour.

**Near-match.** Two purple objects may have different donors. Replacing one with the other can change the answer to “Were these objects donated by different people?” Colour grouping loses information needed to answer that question. The counterexample depends on having two differently donated objects in the same colour group.

**Open interpretation.** The grouping theorem is independent of what a group represents. Applied to Aquinas, it locates a specific consequence of treating sameness of essence as licence to substitute within opposition. The reading of sameness still needs textual and philosophical support. ## Does a necessary divine relation make creation necessary?

**Answer in the model.** The conclusion follows if the proposition about the divine relation holds in every accessible world and the implication from that proposition to creation also holds in every accessible world. Even with two mutually accessible worlds, a bridge asserted only in the actual world permits creation to vary.

**Open interpretation.** The bridge carries the substantive burden. The theorem gives a valid conditional inference in a stated semantics. Its premises require separate defence before drawing a theological conclusion. [Modal investigation](../../corpus/investigations/modal-creation.md)
