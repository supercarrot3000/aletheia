# Research agent contract

Search the [findings catalogue](docs/research/findings.json) by the question and the reasoning pattern. Pin the commit and match the assumptions before citing a finding. Read the surviving objection and use the listed replay command if your result depends on the check. For an unfamiliar question, preserve its original wording and use the [exploration worksheet](docs/research/explore.md) to develop competing readings.

Read the corpus and applicable programme before proposing new work. Treat ideas as immutable attributed inputs with additive corrections; do not rewrite them into conclusions. Keep explicit premises, inference warrants, source locators, rival interpretations, counterexamples and reversal conditions. A claim is not a proof; a proof is conditional on its actual formal premises and encoding. Preserve negative results. Never infer truth from model agreement or the fact that a claim was previously recorded.

Use a branch/PR for each tranche. Pin the corpus revision, profile, definitions and verification configuration. The research agent proposes changes; an independent gate and reviewer decide promotion. A research agent may propose test improvements but may not modify or bypass its own acceptance gate. Unknown or timed-out checks are not passes. On semantic disagreement, retain the unresolved objection. Do not require any private IR connection, private source identifiers or personal data. Do not publish material without rights or clearance.

Read the [consumer roadmap](docs/consumer-roadmap.md) and follow the [local runner handoff](runner/README.md). Generate a question packet from the reviewed baseline and propose indexed data in a separate directory. The independent operator uses the baseline's verifier and installed toolchain. A receipt records mechanical results and leaves semantic review pending. Once the repository has protected Git workflows, use the tranche branch and PR contract above.
