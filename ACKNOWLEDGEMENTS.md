# Acknowledgements

## To the as-yet unidentified creator of the Purple Man story

Many years ago, a contributor heard someone discussing a play they had created, possibly on BBC Radio 2. Its story of a girl in Australia, the colour purple and her longing for Alice Springs was never forgotten. It has continued to move the listener and has become a formative inspiration for this project's investigation of moral beauty, love, moral understanding and the limits of artificial intelligence.

The original creator deserves recognition for that contribution. We hope that one day they, or someone who knows their work, will recognise this story and discover how far its influence has travelled. The project does not claim authorship of the original play or its characters.

The title, author, broadcaster and date remain unconfirmed. See [the complete available recollection](corpus/ideas/purple-man.md) and [the source-identification record](corpus/sources/purple-man-origin.json). The recollection is not a transcript, and its theological interpretation is not presented as verified authorial intent.

If you recognise the work, please provide a verifiable reference (such as a programme listing, script catalogue, interview, production record or confirmation from the creator) through a repository issue once the project is public. Please do not post private contact information or unauthorised copies of the script. Identification should trigger a review of attribution, rights and permissions, and, where feasible and appropriate, an invitation to the creator to learn about the project's development. Retain a record of earlier uncertainty rather than silently replacing it.
